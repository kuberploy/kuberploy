# Community Code of Conduct

Kuberploy is committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still taking security, safety, and impact seriously.
- Welcome newcomers and explain project context without gatekeeping.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or private
  vulnerability report.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, and retaliation are not accepted
in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact method
listed in the repository's Security tab or maintainer profile. Maintainers may
remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or ban a
participant. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical;
conflicts of interest must be disclosed and recused.

This policy applies to repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions,
chat, events, and other spaces where someone is representing Kuberploy.
